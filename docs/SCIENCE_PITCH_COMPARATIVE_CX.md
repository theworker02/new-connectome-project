# Science pitch — comparative CX circuit alignment

## Claim (careful)

Once multi-species **central-complex** EM volumes are publicly available, Insectome can produce **independent** regional connectomes and then build a *mapping layer* of **candidate homologs** — without merging specimens into one biological graph.

## Why CX

- Deep conservation of head-direction / navigation architecture across insects.
- Heinze et al. multi-resolution design explicitly enables comparative reconstruction.
- Regional scope is computationally realistic vs whole-brain claims.

## Mapping products (not merges)

For each pair of species graphs:

- morphology similarity
- region occupancy
- motif / degree similarity
- evidence state: `CANDIDATE_HOMOLOG` vs asserted homology
- full provenance back to EM coordinates

## Gate

No comparative CX release until at least one non-Drosophila CX volume is **downloadable** and Phase B–D validation criteria are met on CREMI (or equivalent GT).
